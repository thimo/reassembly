## Version 1.0

**Promotional text** [170]:

> Shoot while you take something apart, so you know how it went back together.
> Every project is an album in your own Photos library.

**Description** [4000]:

> Re-assembly is the reverse of disassembly.
>
> Re-assembly is a camera for teardown photos. Open a project, shoot every
> step as you take something apart, and when it's time to put it back
> together the photos are right there, in order, newest first.
>
> PHOTOS IS THE DATABASE
> The app stores nothing of its own. Every project is an album in your Photos
> library, folders nest as folders, and iCloud Photos keeps everything in sync
> between your iPhone and iPad for free. Rename, move or delete from the Photos
> app and Re-assembly follows. Delete the app and you lose nothing.
>
> A CAMERA THAT STAYS IN THE PROJECT
> No "Use Photo" confirmation, no picking an album afterwards. The shutter
> saves straight into the open project and the next photo is one tap away.
> Pinch to zoom with lens presets, flash and torch, tap to focus with AE/AF
> lock, grid lines, volume-button shutter, and a quick check of the last shot
> with pinch-zoom.
>
> SMALL THINGS DONE RIGHT
> • Projects sorted by latest activity, folders for clients or bigger jobs
> • Photo grid grouped by day, newest first, multi-select delete
> • Rotate non-destructively; Revert in Photos still works
> • Geotag written on every photo, like the built-in Camera
> • Long-press the app icon, or use Shortcuts, Siri or the Action Button, to
>   jump straight into the camera of your active project
> • English and Dutch
>
> NO ACCOUNT, NO TRACKING, NO SERVER
> Re-assembly collects nothing. Your photos, your library, your business.

**Review notes** (App Review Information):

> Re-assembly stores projects as albums in the Photos library, so it needs
> full Photos access (not Limited): limited access cannot create or list
> albums. The camera and geotag need a real device. To try it: tap "+" to
> create a project, tap the camera button, shoot, swipe down. The photos
> appear in the project grid and in Photos under the "Re-assembly" folder.
> No account or sign-in exists.
